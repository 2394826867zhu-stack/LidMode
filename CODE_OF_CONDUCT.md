# Code of Conduct

LidMode contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and assume good intent while discussing technical decisions.
- Give specific, evidence-based feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect differences in experience, identity, background, and communication style.
- Protect other people's privacy and never publish credentials or personal information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope, security boundaries, and product simplicity.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or repeated hostile conduct is not acceptable in issues, discussions, pull requests, or other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Serious or sensitive conduct concerns should be reported privately to the repository owner rather than posted publicly.

This policy applies to all official LidMode project spaces.
